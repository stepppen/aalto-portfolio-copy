context: |
  Calmea is a medical sleep-tracking platform from ETH's Sensory-Motor Systems Lab. I joined the lab for my civil service and owned its UX/UI from zero to one. Then I went on to build it.

  Calmea needs nothing on the body. A sensor module hidden in the bed frame reads heartbeat and breathing through the structure of the bed (ballistocardiography), and the room around it: temperature, humidity, air quality and light. From that it works out heart rate, breathing rate, sleep stages and movement, every night, with nothing to wear or charge. It's built for bed manufacturers, who put the module into their beds and get an app and service layer with it.
