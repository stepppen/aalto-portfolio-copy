::cs-section{n="01" title="The app"}
The app turns the night's signals into a few things you can read at a glance: a quality score, how long and how regularly you slept, and recommendations in plain words. Day, week and month views show the same night in context.

The same app runs the bed. You switch it on, set intensity and vibration, start a meditation programme and set your bed time.
