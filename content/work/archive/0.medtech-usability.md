  Medical software counts as a medical device when it diagnoses, monitors or treats. That's called SaMD, software as a medical device. Before it can go to market, the maker has to show it can be used safely. A summative usability test is that proof: representative users, realistic tasks, and a close look at every use error that could cause harm.
