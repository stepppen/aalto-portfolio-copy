title: LLM drawing robot
outcome: A drawing robot driven by an LLM and generative AI.
year: "2023"
role: Human-robot interaction, voice UI
org: ZHdK
tier: side
cover:
  src: /TsunComp/thumb.webp
  alt: LLM drawing robot rendering
team: David Polke, Elia Salerno, Andreas Kohler, Stepan Vedunov
timeframe: "2023, three-week university module"
tools: [GPT-3.5, Stable Diffusion, CAD, plotter]
status: Prototype
context: |
  In a three-week module we built a speech-to-drawing machine. You say what you want, and it draws a custom pen illustration for you to take home. It runs on GPT-3.5, Stable Diffusion and a plotter we built ourselves. The point was to explore new ways of interacting with natural language and AI models.
myRole:
  - User research on human-robot interaction.
  - Facilitated ideation rounds.
  - Designed and built the voice-input front end.
  - Industrial design of the robot.
results:
  text: The prototype is a speculative proof of concept for pseudo-sentience in AI. We wanted to learn about the field, and to shape how people interact with robotic systems.
