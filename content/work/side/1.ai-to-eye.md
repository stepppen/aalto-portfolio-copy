  AI to Eye – Between Code and Conscience is a walk-through installation at Scientifica, the science festival of ETH Zurich and the University of Zurich, at ETH Zentrum on Clausiusstrasse. Cameras follow visitors as they move through the space, and light, sound and staging make it feel what it's like to be constantly watched.

  Outside the installation, screens show short statements from researchers, medical professionals, artists, journalists and industry on the chances and risks of AI. The question it leaves you with: what does it mean to remain human in a world shaped by AI?
