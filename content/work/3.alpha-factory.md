::cs-section{n="01" title="A human brand for AI"}
AI brands easily end up cold: dark screens, neon gradients, glowing networks. Alpha Factory is about people adopting AI in their everyday work, so the brand had to feel like it was made by hand. Every element does a bit of that, from the illustrations to the colours to the photos.

The voice is three words. Impactful: a balance between innovation and pragmatism that turns ideas into lasting results. Sophisticated: a trusted partner with high-quality, tailored, Swiss-made work. Lively: energetic, and ready to make it happen.

::cs-section{n="02" title="Logo"}
The logo is an alpha glyph next to the wordmark, drawn as a single ribbon with a gradient. Flat and monochrome versions cover print and places where the gradient can't work.

:::cs-media{src="/AlphaFactory/logo-variants.webp" alt="Four versions of the Alpha Factory logo, in colour and monochrome, on light and dark backgrounds." caption="Logo in multiple variants."}
:::
::

::cs-section{n="03" title="Colours from nature"}
A deep red (Deep AF) leads. Around it sit petal, blush, sage, forest and teal: soft, earthy colours that you'd find outside rather than on a screen. Black, grey and white complement the palette.

:::cs-media{src="/AlphaFactory/palette.webp" alt="The full palette: red, petal, blush, sage, forest, teal, black, grey and white, with hex, CMYK, RGB and Pantone values." caption="The full colour palette."}
:::

::cs-section{n="04" title="One-line illustrations"}
