::cs-section{n="01" title="A human brand for AI"}
AI brands easily end up cold: dark screens, neon gradients, glowing networks. Alpha Factory is about people adopting AI in their everyday work, so the brand had to feel like it was made by hand. Every element does a bit of that, from the illustrations to the colours to the photos.

The voice is three words. Impactful: a balance between innovation and pragmatism that turns ideas into lasting results. Sophisticated: a trusted partner with high-quality, tailored, Swiss-made work. Lively: energetic, and ready to make it happen.

::cs-section{n="03" title="Colours from nature"}
A deep red, Red AF, leads. Around it sit petal, blush, sage, forest and teal: soft, earthy colours that you'd find outside rather than on a screen. Black, grey and white do the rest.

:::cs-media{src="/AlphaFactory/palette.webp" alt="The full palette: red, petal, blush, sage, forest, teal, black, grey and white, with hex, CMYK, RGB and Pantone values." caption="The full palette, with values for screen and print."}
:::

::cs-section{n="04" title="One-line illustrations"}
The illustrations are the clearest sign of the human hand. Each one is a single, continuous monoline stroke in the brand red, with a consistent line width, over a soft blush shape. They look drawn rather than generated.

They support or simplify a key message where a photo or a detailed visual would be too much, and they always sit with a heading, label or text instead of being decoration on their own. A shared library is the single source of truth, so no one redraws them.

:::cs-media{src="/AlphaFactory/illustrations.webp" alt="A grid of one-line illustrations in red, each drawn as a single continuous stroke over a soft blush shape." caption="Part of the illustration library."}
:::

::cs-section{n="05" title="Speed, with something natural in it"}
The imagery is organic, grainy and abstract: images of movement, blurred enough that you can't see what's in them. The motion stands for the speed of AI, and the grain and soft shapes keep it natural. Red is the main background, with teal and two duotones for variety.

:::cs-media{src="/AlphaFactory/imagery.webp" alt="Four blurred, grainy motion images: red, teal, a teal-green duotone and a red duotone." caption="Red, teal and two duotones, used behind headlines."}
:::

::cs-section{n="06" title="Type and icons"}
Space Grotesk sets the titles. Its slightly quirky letterforms balance geometric precision with a humanist warmth, the same mix as the brand. The icons are outlined and rounded.
